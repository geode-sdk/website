# Introduction

Last updated: 2026-09-20

This privacy policy covers what data we (Geode developers) collect, how we use it and how it is protected. This applies to any information collected through your use of:

* This website
* Geode CLI
* Geode mod loader
* Geode launcher for Android / iOS

# What data we collect

## Anonymous telemetry

When you use the Geode mod loader, we automatically collect minor, anonymous technical data to help understand how our software is being used and fix bugs. This includes:
* Geode version
* Operating system/platform
* Geometry Dash game version

This data is entirely anonymous and aggregated, and is used purely for analytical purposes, stored for periods of up to 5 years.

## Developer accounts

If you are a developer and choose to sign in with a GitHub account, we collect:
* Your GitHub username
* Your GitHub user ID

This information is necessary to verify your identity and allow you to publish/manage your mods.

## Website analytics

We use **Google Analytics** on our website to understand how our website is used, and it uses cookies to distinguish users and sessions. It collects:
* the pages you visit and how you navigate between them,
* engagement/session information,
* technical information about your device and browser (device type, operating system, browser),
* approximate location derived from the IP address,
* website that referred you to us.

This data is **not** used to identify you personally, and is processed by Google - see the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Advertising

Certain pages show ads through the **Venatus** ad network. To serve and measure ads, Venatus and its partners may collect your IP address and approximate location, device and browser information, information about the pages you visit, and may set cookies or similar. See the [Venatus Privacy Policy](https://www.venatus.com/privacy-policy).

Advertisements are not shown and their scripts are never loaded if you are logged into an account on the website.

# Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data, and we only share the data needed to operate our services:

* **Google** - website analytics (see above)
* **Venatus and its partners** - ad delivery and measurement (see above)
* **GitHub** - sign-in for developer accounts

# Your rights & data control

We respect your rights under the GDPR, and if you have a developer account on our website, you have the right to:
* Access the data we have about you.
* Request deletion of your developer account and associated data.

To make a request, or if you have any questions or privacy concerns, please contact us at: [admin@geode-sdk.org](mailto:admin@geode-sdk.org)

# Updates to the Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our software, website, or legal requirements. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
